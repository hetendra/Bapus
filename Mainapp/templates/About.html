Our Story

About Bapu's Rotlo

Bringing Royal Heritage of Kutch to Your Plate

Bapu's Rotlo was born from a deep passion to share the authentic, rustic flavors of Kutch, Gujarat with the world. Every recipe is a legacy passed down through generations, prepared with love and the finest ingredients.

Our journey began with a simple belief: food is not just about taste, it's about emotions, traditions, and the warmth of home. We bring you the royal culinary experience of Gujarat, preserving recipes that have stood the test of time.

  • 100% Authentic Gujarati Recipes - Preserved for generations
  • Fresh, Organic Ingredients - Sourced from local farmers of Kutch
  • Traditional Cooking Methods - Taste of home
  • Royal Hospitality - Experience the warmth of Gujarat

Meet the Visionaries Behind Bapu's

Shri Jaydipsinh Jadeja & Smt. Jignaba Jaydipsinh Jadeja

With a shared passion for preserving Gujarat's rich culinary heritage, Shri Jaydipsinh Jadeja and his wife Smt. Jignaba Jaydipsinh Jadeja founded Bapu's Rotlo to bring authentic Kutchi flavors to the world.

Their vision goes beyond just food - it's about creating a legacy of taste, tradition, and togetherness. Under their leadership, Bapu's Rotlo has become a symbol of royal hospitality and authentic Gujarati cuisine.

"Food is not just what we eat, it's an emotion that connects us to our roots." - Jaydipsinh Jadeja
